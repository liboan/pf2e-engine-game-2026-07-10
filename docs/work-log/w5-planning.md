### A. Monk stances — two new L1 feats

Replace Monastic Weaponry on two alternate L1 Monk sheets. Remove kama access/proficiency granted solely by that feat; retain legal baseline statistics, Powerful Fist, Flurry and horizontal Quick Jump. Sources: [Tiger Stance](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=5983), [Wolf Stance](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=5984), [Backstabber](https://2e.aonprd.com/Traits.aspx?ID=544).

Both stances cost one action, require unarmored, use the existing stance lifecycle and make their special attack available only while active. Unlike Crane, neither forbids ordinary fist Strikes. Tiger grants agile/finesse/nonlethal 1d8 slashing claws; a damaging critical hit adds exactly 1d4 persistent bleed (not doubled again). It permits a 10-foot Step only with effective Speed at least 20. Wolf grants agile/finesse/nonlethal 1d8 piercing jaws, +1 precision against off-guard, and the trip trait only when actually flanking; off-guard from Feint alone does not grant trip. Apply existing rune and precision-immunity mechanisms.

Verified seams: `martial_defense.py` currently hard-codes Crane exclusivity in `crane_stance_attack_permitted`; `model.MartialStanceState`, `Encounter._attack_usable`, `_step`, `_step_destinations`, `_is_flanked`, `_apply_persistent_effect`, `skill_actions.Trip`, `_maneuver_weapon`, and `monk.is_flurry_strike`/`paired_strikes.py`. **Backstabber is not implemented today; neither is unarmed maneuver selection.** `Trip` currently accepts only `maneuver_item_id`; append an optional unarmed `maneuver_attack_id` without breaking positional callers, forbid simultaneous selectors, and validate live stance/flanking at commitment and resume. Do not invent an item for a jaw.

Own new `monk_stances.py`, `w5_stance_content.py`, `w5_stance_terminal.py`, `w5_stance_persistence.py`. Extract the existing Crane-specific attack gate into a finite stance policy used by old and new stances; leave parry ownership to B. For Tiger Step, append optional path input to `Step` (legacy destination calls still work), use the existing `_begin_move` legality/diagonal/occupancy pipeline, and expose only bounded one/two-square legal paths. A two-square destination must never jump an occupied intermediate cell.

Persistent damage already supports dice and recovery, but its installation helper only compares equal dice shapes and the loader assumes spell/alchemy provenance. Extract that finite installation/validation logic to `persistent_effects.py`, with explicit Tiger provenance. For ambiguous 1d4 bleed versus existing flat bleed, offer a saved, bounded GM choice of existing/incoming source, as [Persistent Damage](https://2e.aonprd.com/Conditions.aspx?ID=86) requires; do not silently average or reject after committing damage. Keep the existing one-minute GM convention. Check actual post-mitigation damage before adding Tiger bleed, including shield/reaction continuations. No new universal condition framework.
