# Selected Investigator: knowledge, medicine and planned attacks

## Decision and delivery boundary

Astra confirmed the approved **Forensic Medicine + Known Weaknesses** build. Keep it: Forensic Acumen already requires the same small knowledge procedure, so replacing the optional feat would remove little work. This packet is researched design, not implemented class coverage. Work follows the selected Sorcerer and Justice Champion.

Use literal character facts and authored scenario knowledge. Reuse saved checks, typed bonuses, precision damage, health, hands, elapsed-time cooldowns and turn boundaries. Do not add a general investigation or dialogue framework.

## Required level 1 behavior

[Investigator](https://2e.aonprd.com/Classes.aspx?ID=59) uses Intelligence, 8 + Constitution HP per class level, expert Perception/Reflex/Will, and trained Fortitude, attacks, light/unarmored defense and class DC. Include Society, methodology Medicine and the selected legal additional skills.

**Pursue a Lead** examines a clue for a minute, with explicit judgment about its mystery. Support two investigations, the ten-minute cooldown, replacement/abandonment and the daily-preparation restriction. Relevant checks receive +1 circumstance. **Clue In** spends a reaction before another creature's relevant check, grants the investigation bonus, and has its own ten-minute cooldown.

[Devise a Stratagem](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2813) stores a d20 and then offers attack or skill mode, once per round. It normally costs an action; a known investigation subject permits the printed free use. The first Strike against that subject uses the stored die with current MAP and modifiers. Intelligence substitution is optional and must be eligible for the weapon. Strategic Strike adds 1d6 precision only when Intelligence is actually used. Skill mode instead restricts target Strikes and benefits the next applicable mental skill or Perception check.

The preliminary die is not a check. A substituted Strike uses fortune; neither is eligible for another Hero Point reroll. Preserve this through saves, without drawing a fresh attack die. A normal unrelated check can still use a Hero Point. [Hero Points](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2333), [fortune](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=2263).

[Known Weaknesses](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=5936) offers actual Recall Knowledge before Devise's die. Critical success grants each informed ally's next attack against the subject +1 circumstance until the investigator's next turn; the attack stratagem also benefits. Use normal bonus stacking, not another flat addition.

[Forensic Medicine](https://2e.aonprd.com/Methodologies.aspx?ID=7) grants [Battle Medicine](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=5125). The trained DC 15 option heals 2d8 on success or 4d8 on critical success, plus the investigator's level. Critical failure deals 1d8 damage. Preserve wounded. Track the one-hour immunity by medic and recipient, including failed attempts. A worn [healer's toolkit](https://2e.aonprd.com/Equipment.aspx?ID=2727) needs one usable hand; holding it needs two. Its manipulate action must allow actual reactions.

[Forensic Acumen](https://2e.aonprd.com/Feats.aspx?ID=6483) halves an examination's time, to a minimum of five minutes. Success permits immediate relevant Recall Knowledge with +2 circumstance.

## Bounded GM judgment interface

An authored record declares the subject/question, approved skill and DC, investigation relevance, examination duration, information for each outcome, communication and prior attempts. Recall Knowledge still rolls and resolves; merely declaring knowledge does not activate the feat. Persist [repetition limits](https://2e.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=563&Redirected=1). Use an explicitly declared GM-open-roll presentation permitted by the secret-check rules. Missing necessary context rejects before costs. [Recall Knowledge](https://2e.aonprd.com/Actions.aspx?ID=2367).

The author supplies judgments the rules assign to a GM. The engine handles the resulting checks, bonuses, restrictions, costs and clocks. This does not claim automatic inference about arbitrary mysteries or creature knowledge.

## Four proposed public encounters

Example modifiers: Int +4, Dex +3, Wis +1, Str +0; shortsword +6 normally/+7 with Intelligence, Medicine +4, Society/Crafting +7. Workers must turn these into legal concrete initial builds and finish each authored scene through public actions.

1. **Clue room:** a five-minute examination rolls 11 + 4 = 15. Save before immediate Crafting follow-up; 12 + 7 + 2 = 21. Pursue the resulting lead and Clue In an ally's relevant check: 11 + 3 + 1 = 15. Save the cooldowns.
2. **Two foes:** Known Weaknesses totals 18 + 7 + 1 = 26 against authored DC 16. Store Devise 14 and save. Attack another foe first; the agile shortsword attack against the chosen subject then totals 14 + 7 + 1 − 4 = 18, with weapon 4 + precision 3 = 7 damage. No new attack die or Hero prompt.
3. **Poor stratagem:** store 3, choose skill mode and save. Reject a Strike at that subject; a fresh relevant Society check totals 10 + 7 + 2 = 19. Consume its bonus once and end the restriction at the correct turn.
4. **Rescue:** an initially healthy 20-HP ally takes 10 damage through play. Battle Medicine totals 11 + 4 = 15 and heals 3 + 4 + 1 = 8, reaching 18 HP. Save immunity; reject a repeat before 3,600 seconds and permit it at expiry.
