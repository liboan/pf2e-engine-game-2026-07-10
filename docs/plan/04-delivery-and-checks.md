## Scope and brief feasibility

Prioritize materially broader genuinely new playable content, especially level-1 class feats across accepted classes. Planning ambition is roughly a dozen distinct new feats across the lanes, plus spells/items where equally inexpensive reuse is useful. This is a scope target, not a count quota: alternate class configurations of existing names, definitions, placeholders and unselected candidates do not count as new content. W4 must materially exceed W3's five distinct feats and two items while preserving correctness. Do not stop after one small option; if the feasible roster cannot support that ambition, send the smallest substantive scope decision rather than pad delivery.

Each owner briefly checks a related candidate set against the accepted catalog and applicable sources, selects its actual roster, records legal grants/material differences/reusable helpers/deferred prerequisites in a scoped note, then immediately implements. No approval per feat and no exhaustive inventory. Review completed runnable groups. Lane definitions and startup pointers are in [W4 handoff](07-content-w4-handoff.md). Source-check candidate names before commitment. Content may be chosen within existing L1/L2 menus; do not raise representative levels or admit unsupported behavior silently.

Reuse established mechanics and proactively simplify touched branching. Split oversized mixed-responsibility modules cohesively, extract shared behavior with real adopters and delete replaced paths before or during extension. Keep refactors proportional to playable delivery. No infrastructure-only wave or speculative universal framework. Each shared abstraction has one named owner and a concrete API/semantic contract. Coordinate dependency changes directly with affected peers; independent useful work stays parallel. Avoid simultaneous wholesale rewrites of encounter/persistence/terminal; owners extract narrowly scoped family seams, integration lead resolves overlap deliberately.
